Candidates identified but not executed
--------------------------------------

The Don Maslin index identifies SuperCalc, Perfect Calc, dBASE II, Perfect
Filer, VanData accounting, PBBS, and printer utilities. During this
investigation, direct retrieval returned an authenticated HTML login page
rather than the named ZIP files. RetroArchive independently identifies dBASE
II and public-domain BBS families, but its download endpoint was unavailable
to the experimental host. No bytes from those failed acquisitions are retained
or claimed as software evidence.

Consequently spreadsheet, database, packaged business, BBS, and dedicated
printer-workflow execution remain open. Identification is documentation only,
not an experimental compatibility result.
