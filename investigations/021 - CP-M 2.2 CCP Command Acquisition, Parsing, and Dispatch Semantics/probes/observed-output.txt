Investigation 021 accepted observations - 15 August 2026
========================================================

Environment: z80pack cpmsim 1.39, commit
91fd28eb04e675c2127df88ed3f40675e15282e2; 64K DRI CP/M 2.2; Z80 CBIOS 1.2.

Acquisition and whitespace
--------------------------
- CR only, one SPACE, and several SPACEs each produced another A> prompt with
  no error.
- TAB-only and SPACE/TAB/SPACE were not empty: the common parser printed the
  offending control input followed by `?` and resumed at A>.
- Multiple leading SPACEs before DIR were ignored.
- Lowercase `dir` and mixed-case `DiR` dispatched resident DIR; the console
  echo preserved typed case while CCP processing normalized it.
- A 127-byte X command was accepted and then reported with `?`.

Drive forms
-----------
- A: while on A was a no-op prompt transition.
- B: changed the following prompt to B>; A: changed it back to A>.
- B:DIR did not dispatch resident DIR. It temporarily loaded B:DIR.COM,
  printed the MARK21 marker, then returned to A>.
- B:NOEXIST printed `B:NOEXIST?` and returned to A>.
- Q: entered `Bdos Err On Q: Select`; deterministic `x` caused warm restart
  and A>.
- :FOO was treated as an empty command; AA:, FOO:BAR, and FOO;BAR reached
  generic error handling with their token followed by `?`.

Recognition and precedence
--------------------------
- Exact built-in DIR won over A:DIR.COM and printed the directory.
- DIR.COM also selected resident DIR; explicit `.COM` did not force transient.
- TYPE.COM selected the resident TYPE recognition path, then failed its
  operand processing with `?`; MARK21 did not execute.
- DI and DIRX were not abbreviations and failed as transient names.
- FOO.TXT was rejected; the normal transient command position takes a primary
  name and CCP supplies COM internally.
- The DRI source table contains exactly DIR, ERA, TYPE, SAVE, REN, USER.

Transient-entry observations
----------------------------
`PARSE21 one two` produced uppercase FCB names ONE/TWO and tail bytes
`20 4F 4E 45 20 54 57 4F` (" ONE TWO").

`parse21   a:b *.com` produced FCB1 drive 1/name B, FCB2 wildcard name/type
????????.COM, and tail `20 20 20 41 3A 42 20 2A 2E 43 4F 4D`.

This strengthens the DRI-CCP evidence for I001 but does not, by itself,
convert every source-specific I001 policy choice into a universal contract.

Preservation
------------
Fixture and after hashes are identical for both drives. No command in the
accepted matrix changed either disk after installation of the controlled COM
files.
