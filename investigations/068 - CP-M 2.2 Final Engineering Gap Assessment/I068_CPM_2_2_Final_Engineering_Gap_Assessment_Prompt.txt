specification.

- software ecosystem compatibility requirements;

closed the identified gaps.

- whether additional investigations are required.

INVESTIGATION QUESTIONS


1. GAP CLOSURE REVIEW

Determine:

- whether every I060 gap was addressed;
- whether each closure investigation produced sufficient evidence;
- whether unresolved issues remain.


2. COMPATIBILITY LEDGER REVIEW

Determine:

- whether classifications remain correct;
- whether new requirements were discovered;
- whether requirements should be revised;
- whether duplicate or conflicting entries exist.


3. SOFTWARE ECOSYSTEM REVIEW

Determine:

- whether the expanded corpus provides sufficient coverage;
- whether important software categories remain missing;
- whether software evidence supports the compatibility contract.


4. CROSS-LAYER REVIEW

Determine:

- whether interactions between subsystems are adequately covered;
- whether compatibility boundaries remain clear;
- whether hidden assumptions remain.


5. CONFORMANCE REVIEW

Determine:

- whether conformance testing is sufficient;
- whether requirements map to validation procedures;
- whether an independent implementation could demonstrate compliance.


6. HARDWARE AND PROFILE REVIEW

Determine:

- whether generic CP/M compatibility is clearly separated from hardware
  profiles;
- whether profile requirements are evidence-based;
- whether machine-specific behavior has been correctly classified.


7. SPECIFICATION READINESS

Determine:

- whether the CP/M 2.2 compatibility specification is ready for release;
- whether additional investigations are necessary;
- what final conditions remain before specification freeze.
