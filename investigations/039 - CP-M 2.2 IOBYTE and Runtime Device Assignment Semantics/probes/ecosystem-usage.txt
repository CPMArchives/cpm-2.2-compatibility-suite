IOBYTE / DEVICE-ASSIGNMENT SOFTWARE EVIDENCE

Documented interface
  The Alteration Guide defines location 0003h as four two-bit fields, explicitly
  says assignments may be altered during CP/M processing, and points to STAT.

DRI STAT.PLM
  Reads/writes 0003h directly. DEV: displays all four fields. Assignment parsing
  recognizes CON/RDR/PUN/LST and the DRI physical names TTY/CRT/BAT/UC1,
  TTY/PTR/UR1/UR2, TTY/PTP/UP1/UP2, and TTY/CRT/LPT/UL1. It masks only the chosen
  field and prints Invalid Assignment for an unknown name.

Ecosystem boundary
  Batch/console switching, printer routing, reader/punch transfer, and terminal or
  communications utilities have a documented reason to inspect or change IOBYTE.
  The byte/field semantics are portable; exact DRI names and actual attached devices
  are machine/profile conventions. No quantitative local corpus supports additional
  vendor names or private routing caches.
