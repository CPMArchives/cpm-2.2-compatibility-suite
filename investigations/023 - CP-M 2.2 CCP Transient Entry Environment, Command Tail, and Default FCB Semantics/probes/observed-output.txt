Investigation 023 accepted observations
=======================================

Accepted evidence: transcripts/console-main.txt, console-repeat.txt,
console-max.txt, and console-usercheck.txt.

1. Tail layout
   - no arguments: 0080=00 and 0081=00;
   - `ENTRY23 ARG`: 04, SPACE, ARG, NUL;
   - `ENTRY23 ARG1 ARG2`: 0A, SPACE, ARG1, SPACE, ARG2, NUL;
   - three separators before ARG are preserved and counted;
   - CR is neither counted nor copied;
   - bytes after the NUL are residual and differ between fresh runs.

2. Case and character handling
   Lowercase input is visibly echoed as entered, but ASCII a-z in both tail and
   FCBs is uppercase at entry. Digits, hyphen, underscore, `*`, and `?` survive.

3. Tail capacity
   With the one-character alias `T`, a 127-byte input line (`T`, space, 125
   argument bytes) produces tail count 7Eh (126). An attempted 128th byte is not
   included; it remains queued and becomes the next command (`B?`). No bell is
   present in the captured transcript. This is a CCP input-line limit, not an
   independent 127-byte tail guarantee.

4. FCB construction
   The first two operands form uppercase, space-padded prefixes at 005Ch and
   006Ch. Unprefixed operands have drive 0 even on current drive B; A: and B:
   encode 1 and 2. `*` fills the remaining name/type positions with `?`, while
   literal `?` remains. Long names are truncated to eight characters in the FCB
   but remain complete in the tail.

5. FCB residual state
   EX/S1/S2 and the first CR byte at 007Ch are zero. First-FCB byte 15 is 08 for
   ENTRY23 and 14h for 20-record BIG23, exposing loader/Open state rather than a
   constructed zero. Bytes 007Dh-007Fh and bytes after the tail NUL vary or
   retain prior contents. The second operand supplies only a 16-byte prefix;
   its position necessarily overlays the first FCB allocation area.

6. Drive and user
   Page-zero 0004h is 00 for user 0/drive A, 10h for user 1/drive A, and 01h
   for user 0/drive B. BDOS Functions 32 and 25 independently report matching
   user and drive. The command's explicit lookup drive (`B:ENTRY23`) does not
   change current drive and does not become an argument FCB drive.

7. DMA, stack, vectors, registers
   Reading DMACHK.DAT without Function 26 succeeds and deposits its signature
   at 0080h, confirming initial DMA 0080h. Entry SP EBA9h contains EB5Fh and RET
   returns to CCP in this configuration. Exact SP/return/vector targets and
   initial registers vary or are configuration-specific and are not portable.
