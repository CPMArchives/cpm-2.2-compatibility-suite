INVESTIGATION 053 CORPUS COVERAGE

Covered categories
- Standard software: DRI ASM, LOAD, PIP, STAT, ED, DDT, SUBMIT, XSUB, DUMP.
- Development software: ASM, MAC, RMAC, LOAD, LINK, DDT, SID, SUBMIT.
- Applications: WordStar 3.00, Microsoft BASIC-80 5.2/Wumpus, Adventure A02.
- Communications: Kermit-80 Generic CP/M 4.11.
- Hardware-dependent: IMSAI-patched QTERM 4.3e and Cromemco KSCOPE, with BASE051 control.

Operation classes
- Normal: utility/toolchain workflows, WordStar save, BASIC and Adventure runs,
  Kermit startup, BASE051.
- Boundary: multi-record/multi-module builds, large application, alternate terminal
  and logical-device profiles, direct-port traps.
- Failure: missing source/input, undefined link symbol, unsupported Kermit speed,
  missing send file, absent peer abort, unsupported hardware ports.

Material gaps
- Spreadsheet, database, packaged business, and BBS applications.
- Successful paired communications transfer and receive-side/carrier failures.
- Matching IMSAI/Dazzler and other named hardware profiles.
- Printer workflows and high-level compiled languages beyond BASIC/assembly tools.

These are corpus/profile gaps. They are not evidence that the current baseline
contract is deficient.
