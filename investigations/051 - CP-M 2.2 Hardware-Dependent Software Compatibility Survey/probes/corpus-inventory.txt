HARDWARE-DEPENDENT SOFTWARE CORPUS

BASE051
  Control probe; public CP/M BDOS only; executed T01.

QTERM 4.3e, IMSAI 8080 / VT100 patch
  Terminal/modem software; IMSAI SIO-2 ports 22h-25h and 4 MHz patch;
  source and executable preserved; unsupported-profile test T02.

KSCOPE, Cromemco Dazzler
  Display demonstration; Dazzler ports 0Eh/0Fh and interrupt assumptions;
  source and executable preserved; unsupported-profile test T03.

VI/Open, IMSAI VIO version
  Full-screen editor; memory-mapped VIO at F000h, ports 6Eh/6Fh, and dynamic
  BIOS-vector discovery; source and executable preserved; mismatch test T04.

PROMMER 2.0 distribution / patch area 1.0
  EPROM-programmer utility; base port 14h, 4 MHz timing loops, interrupt mode,
  and terminal patches; source and executable preserved; T05 inconclusive.

Altair FDCT1 and KILLBITS
  Standalone controller/front-panel utilities; direct ports 8/9 and FFh;
  source preserved and not executed as CP/M COM programs.

IMSAI CP/M 2.2 BIOS
  Configured BIOS reference; direct serial, printer, interrupt controller,
  FDC, LEDs, firmware signatures, and memory-mapped descriptor assumptions;
  source evidence only, not an application.
