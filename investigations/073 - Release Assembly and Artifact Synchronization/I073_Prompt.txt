Investigation 073 Prompt

Title

Release Assembly and Artifact Synchronization

Purpose

Investigation 073 continues from I072, which established that the CP/M
compatibility boundary, proposition ledger, evidence chain, and
conformance framework are stable.

I073 is not a compatibility investigation. It must not reopen CP/M
behavior research, alter proposition dispositions, renumber ledger
entries, or modify the compatibility contract.

The purpose of I073 is to assemble the release artifacts required to
transition the frozen specification set into a coherent release package.

Scope

I073 shall examine:

1.  Release documentation assembly

-   Confirm required release documents exist.
-   Confirm document naming, versioning, and cross-references.
-   Identify missing publication artifacts.

2.  Conformance package preparation

-   Verify that the frozen conformance framework can be published.
-   Identify required conformance documentation, test descriptions, and
    implementation guidance.

3.  Registry and manifest synchronization

-   Confirm that release manifests accurately describe included
    artifacts.
-   Confirm that identifiers and references are synchronized across
    documents.

4.  Historical integrity

-   Preserve the frozen compatibility ledger.
-   Do not modify proposition blocks.
-   Do not alter evidence or dispositions except through a separately
    authorized investigation.

Non-Goals

I073 shall not:

-   introduce new CP/M compatibility propositions;
-   reopen previous investigations;
-   change compatibility decisions;
-   revise the ledger structure;
-   redefine conformance requirements;
-   redesign architecture.

Any discovered issue requiring those actions must be recorded as a
future investigation item.

Required Outputs

I073 shall produce:

-   a release assembly assessment;
-   an artifact inventory;
-   identification of missing release components;
-   verification of document synchronization;
-   any required follow-up investigations.

Completion Criteria

I073 is complete when:

-   the release package contents are identified;
-   all required artifacts have owners or creation plans;
-   the frozen compatibility record remains unchanged;
-   remaining work is limited to release engineering and publication
    tasks.
