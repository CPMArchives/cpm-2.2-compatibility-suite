CHARACTER-DEVICE SOFTWARE USAGE EVIDENCE

Documented named consumers
  The CP/M 2.2 Alteration Guide says BDOS needs CONST/CONIN/CONOUT, PIP may use
  LIST/PUNCH/READER, and DESPOOL used LISTST. These are primary documentary
  examples of software-visible character devices beyond the console.

DRI source consumers
  OS3BDOS uses CONST/CONIN/CONOUT for console paths and dispatches Functions 3,
  4 and 5 to READER/PUNCH/LIST. OS2CCP uses BDOS console services rather than
  depending on private BIOS internals.

Category conclusions
  Editors and development tools rely on documented console editing/status paths.
  Transfer utilities may use reader/punch. Printing/spooling software uses LIST
  and may poll LISTST. Communications and terminal programs commonly need raw
  console behavior, for which Function 6 and direct BIOS are the compatible
  boundaries already evidenced by I004/I020/I036.

Evidence limit
  The local corpus does not quantify third-party programs or justify requiring
  a particular serial timing, printer-ready polarity, absent-device stub, modem
  convention, or vendor port. Those require named platform profiles.
