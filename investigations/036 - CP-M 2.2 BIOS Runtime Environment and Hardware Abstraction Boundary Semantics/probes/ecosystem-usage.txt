LOCAL CORPUS: DIRECT BIOS / SYSTEM-BOUNDARY USAGE

DRI SYSGEN.ASM
  Defines SELDSK through WRITE as offsets relative to the page-zero WBOOT
  target. At runtime it reads that target, adds the required offset, and jumps
  directly to BIOS disk entries. This is primary source evidence that direct
  BIOS disk access is required for at least DRI system-generation software.

DRI XSUB1.ASM
  Reads and temporarily replaces the WBOOT vector operand, restores it later,
  and manages DMA through BDOS. This demonstrates historical system utility
  dependence on documented page-zero/system conventions, while also showing
  that vector mutation is specialized and must be restored.

CP/M 2.2 Alteration Guide
  States that PIP may use LIST, PUNCH, and READER even though BDOS does not need
  them, and that LISTST was used by DESPOOL. This is documentary evidence of
  direct optional-device consumers.
