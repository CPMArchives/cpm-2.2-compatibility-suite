INVESTIGATION 060 GAP-ASSESSMENT ARTIFACTS

- gap-assessment-matrix.tsv: 24 areas with coverage, evidence, gap, severity,
  action and release classification.
- recommended-investigations.tsv: seven evidence campaigns prioritized by claim impact.
- release-gates.tsv: ten explicit normative/profile/corpus release conditions.
- evidence-review-summary.tsv: strengths and limitations by evidence class.
- duplicate-ledger-audit.tsv: mechanical duplicate identifier/disposition audit.
- policy-pending-inventory.tsv: all 50 pending proposition lines.
- conformance-test-inventory.tsv, ledger-test-traceability.tsv,
  software-corpus-records.tsv and undocumented-behavior-inventory.tsv: source inputs.
- generate-ledger-gap-audits.awk: deterministic duplicate/policy extractor.
- validate060.sh and validation-audit.txt: structural/completeness checks.
- ledger hashes, protected-file audit and SHA256SUMS: preservation evidence.

This assessment challenges specification completeness. It does not implement or
design BetterCP/M and does not convert recommendations into compatibility rules.
