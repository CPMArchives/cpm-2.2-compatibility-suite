CONFORMANCE EVIDENCE REQUIREMENTS

For every run preserve:
- authoritative ledger and test-inventory hashes;
- implementation build identity and declared conformance/profile manifest;
- test source and executable hashes;
- restored before fixtures and after-state hashes;
- deterministic input and timing/repetition policy;
- raw console/BIOS transcript;
- registers, memory, FCB, DMA and call activity where relevant;
- disk images or byte/sector/directory diffs for mutations;
- structured result record and failure classification;
- manual-review record for visual, physical, destructive or profile cases.

Evidence is invalid for certification if expected results are reconstructed from
source after an unperformed test, mutable fixtures are not restored, raw output is
discarded, or profile applicability cannot be reproduced.
