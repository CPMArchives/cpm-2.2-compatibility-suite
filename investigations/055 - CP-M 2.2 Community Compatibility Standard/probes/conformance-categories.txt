INVESTIGATION 055 CONFORMANCE CATEGORIES

1. Baseline CP/M 2.2
   All applicable REQUIRED ledger propositions for a standard CP/M 2.2 system:
   entry/page zero, CCP, BDOS, FCB/DMA/storage, configured BIOS, memory/lifecycle,
   and documented error/recovery behavior. A baseline claim cannot silently omit
   a REQUIRED proposition merely because the implementation is different.

2. Strict community ecosystem
   The baseline plus REQUIRED ecosystem behavior whose necessity is established
   by historical software, notably DRI SUBMIT/XSUB interoperation and writable/
   executable application-owned TPA. This is still a requirements category, not
   permission to freeze private DRI internals.

3. Optional compatibility profile
   A named terminal, console-presentation, IOBYTE/device, communications, error-
   presentation, extension, or policy selection. POLICY PENDING propositions do
   not become passing baseline requirements until the profile is selected and
   its evidence boundary is stated.

4. Hardware-profile compatibility
   A named BIOS/machine/device profile with configured geometry, character paths,
   ports, MMIO, controller or peripheral behavior. Generic CP/M compatibility
   does not imply every historical machine. A matching-profile claim activates
   its applicable tests; a mismatched direct access is not a baseline failure.

5. Outside/unsupported behavior
   NOT REQUIRED private mechanisms and behaviors outside the declared profile.
   NOT GUARANTEED items are not features in this category: they are explicit
   variability constraints that tests and applications must not over-assert.

Claim syntax (conceptual)
   CP/M 2.2 baseline + [strict community] + [named optional profiles] +
   [named hardware profiles], with versioned ledger/test/evidence identifiers.
