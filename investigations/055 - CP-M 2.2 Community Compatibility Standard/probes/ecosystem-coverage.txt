COMMUNITY ECOSYSTEM COVERAGE REVIEW

Validated coverage
- DRI standard utilities and resident commands.
- Assembly, macro assembly, linking, loading, debugging and submitted builds.
- WordStar 3.00, BASIC-80/Wumpus and Adventure A02 application workflows.
- Generic Kermit startup and controlled failure paths.
- Portable public-interface controls and unsupported QTERM/KSCOPE hardware traps.
- Documented, de facto and selected undocumented behaviors through I054.

The model explains every observed successful workflow and expected failure in the
I053 corpus without adding a baseline requirement. I054 found no contradiction.

Material uncovered or incomplete categories
- Spreadsheet, database, packaged business, BBS and printer workflows.
- Additional compiled languages beyond assembly and BASIC.
- Successful paired communications transfer, receive errors and carrier loss.
- Matching IMSAI, Dazzler and other hardware/peripheral profiles.
- Some timing-sensitive console/serial and late physical-fault cases.

These remain corpus/profile gaps. They do not invalidate the baseline standard,
but a claim covering one of them requires named software, fixtures and evidence.
