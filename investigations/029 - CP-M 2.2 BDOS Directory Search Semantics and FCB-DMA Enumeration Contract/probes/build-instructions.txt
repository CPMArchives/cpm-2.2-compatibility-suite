Prerequisites:
  z80asm (Release 2.1)
  cpmtools with ibm-3740 format
  expect
  cpmsim (Release 1.39)

Commands from probes/:
  ./build.sh
  ./make-fixtures.sh cases/recreated
  ./run-main029.exp cases/main transcripts/main.txt
  ./run-error029.exp cases/error transcripts/error.txt

build.sh expands COMMON29.INC into temporary assembler input and preserves
the original ASM, binary COM, and assembler listing (.lis) for every probe.
