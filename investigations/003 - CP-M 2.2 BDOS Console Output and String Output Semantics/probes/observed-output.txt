Reference command:

  A>OUT003

Observed console text (spacing preserved):

OUT003 BEGIN
F2:A$   X

F9:A    X
F9TERM:VISIBLE:AFTER
EMPTY:[]
MULTI:A
B

Interpretation of discriminating lines:

  F2:A$   X       function 2 emitted '$'; TAB after column 5 became 3 spaces
  F9:A    X       function 9 TAB after column 4 became 4 spaces
  F9TERM...       first '$' and following HIDDEN were not emitted; later call emitted :AFTER
  EMPTY:[]        function 9 with '$' as its first byte emitted nothing
  MULTI:A / B     embedded CR/LF were emitted and scanning continued

Printer-echo experiment:

At the CCP prompt, control-P was entered before OUT003 to enable DRI BDOS
printer echo. The z80pack printer sink captured printer-echo.txt. Its probe
segment duplicates the fully processed console stream, including expanded
TAB spaces. z80pack deliberately suppresses CR bytes in its printer-file
handler (simio.c 1503-1514), so that omission is emulator behavior rather
than a CP/M/BDOS observation.
