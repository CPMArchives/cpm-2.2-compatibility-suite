Recommended controlled-ledger treatment
=======================================

The I070 release-applicability classes need not replace the canonical ledger's
four historical dispositions. During the separately authorized update:

  REQUIRED          -> change Disposition to REQUIRED.
  PROFILE REQUIRED  -> rewrite as a conditional, independently testable
                       proposition ("A claim selecting profile X shall...") and
                       set Disposition to REQUIRED.
  OPTIONAL          -> set baseline Disposition to NOT REQUIRED and state the
                       optional profile/extension applicability explicitly.
  NOT GUARANTEED    -> change Disposition to NOT GUARANTEED.
  OUTSIDE SCOPE     -> change Disposition to NOT REQUIRED and state that no
                       BetterCP/M CP/M compatibility claim promises it.

This preserves machine-readable REQUIRED/NOT GUARANTEED/NOT REQUIRED meanings
while eliminating all POLICY PENDING entries. Profile manifests determine which
conditional REQUIRED propositions apply to a conformance claim.

Counts:
  6  direct REQUIRED changes
  28 conditional profile-scoped REQUIRED rewrites
  3  OPTIONAL -> baseline NOT REQUIRED with explicit opt-in metadata
  11 NOT GUARANTEED changes
  1  OUTSIDE SCOPE -> NOT REQUIRED with explicit exclusion

Overlaps:
  0012 and 0506 share one strict-CCP policy and may share a test, but retain
  distinct propositions (entry environment versus operand assignment).
  0435 owns Function 37 adoption; 0523 is its conditional state-effect detail.
  Both receive the same optional-profile applicability and are not merged.
