Investigation 069 — CP/M 2.2 Compatibility Ledger Integrity Resolution

Objective:
Resolve remaining structural integrity issues in the Compatibility Ledger
before normative release preparation.

Scope:
Audit the current authoritative Compatibility Ledger and produce a clean,
canonical ledger state suitable for subsequent policy resolution,
conformance finalization, and release-candidate preparation.

Required Activities:

1. Ledger Identifier Integrity

   Audit all numbered ledger identifiers.

   Identify and resolve:
   - duplicated ledger identifiers;
   - missing identifiers;
   - inconsistent numbering;
   - references to identifiers that do not exist.

   Preserve the meaning and history of existing compatibility
   propositions.

   Do not silently delete, merge, or rewrite propositions without
   documenting the disposition.

2. Investigation 063 Processor Propositions

   Review the five proposed processor-profile propositions from
   Investigation 063:

   - proposed entries 0766–0770.

   Determine the appropriate ledger disposition for each proposition:

   Possible outcomes:
   - add as numbered ledger propositions;
   - classify as profile requirements outside the generic CP/M baseline;
   - classify as POLICY PENDING;
   - classify as NOT REQUIRED or NOT GUARANTEED.

   The decision must preserve the layered compatibility model:

       Generic CP/M
           |
       Processor Profile
           |
       BIOS/Device Profile
           |
       Machine Profile
           |
       Application/Ecosystem Profile

   A machine or processor profile may add requirements but may not waive
   generic CP/M compatibility requirements.

3. Ledger Consistency Review

   Verify that:
   - all existing required propositions remain represented;
   - evidence references remain valid;
   - disposition classifications remain internally consistent;
   - cross-investigation references resolve correctly.

4. Canonical Ledger Production

   Produce a corrected authoritative Compatibility Ledger.

   Requirements:
   - preserve existing formatting;
   - preserve existing proposition text unless a documented correction is
     required;
   - document all changes made;
   - maintain cumulative investigation history.

   The resulting ledger must be the authoritative baseline for:
   - Investigation 070 — Policy Pending Resolution;
   - Investigation 071 — Conformance Framework Finalization;
   - Investigation 072 — CP/M 2.2 Compatibility Standard Release Candidate.

Validation Requirements:

- Complete identifier audit.
- Duplicate identifier report.
- Missing identifier report.
- Processor proposition disposition report.
- Before/after ledger comparison.
- SHA-256 manifest of generated artifacts.
- Confirmation that no unrelated BetterCP/M files were modified.

Expected Outcome:

A clean canonical Compatibility Ledger with all known structural issues
resolved and all processor-profile propositions assigned an explicit
compatibility disposition.
