Investigation 063 source analysis

Digital Research source corpus
------------------------------
The preserved reconstructed CP/M 2.2 corpus at cpm2-plm uses Intel 8080
mnemonics throughout the inspected CCP, BDOS, BIOS, assembler, debugger and
utility modules. A case-insensitive mnemonic-boundary scan of all *.ASM files
for IX, IY, EXX, DJNZ, JR, LDIR, LDDR, CPIR, CPDR, INIR, INDR, OTIR and OTDR
returned no matches. This supports an 8080-compatible DRI implementation
baseline. It does not prove the absence of encoded data or characterize every
third-party program.

z80pack execution implementation
--------------------------------
cpmsim 1.39 exposes separate -8 (Intel 8080) and -z (Zilog Z80) execution
modes, an optional -u trap for undocumented instructions, and -f clock-rate
selection. These controls allow the same CP/M image and program bytes to be
compared without substituting source reconstruction for observation.

Boundary interpretation
-----------------------
8080 instruction semantics are CPU-visible binary semantics, distinct from
BDOS residual register state. The ledger's existing entries 0032, 0033, 0049,
and 0051 correctly reject initial/residual Z80 registers and flags as CP/M
call-contract guarantees. They do not excuse an advertised processor profile
from implementing its own documented instruction results and flags.

Z80 documented extensions are consequential for Z80-targeted software, but
the available DRI CP/M source and T01-T03 do not justify making them universal
CP/M requirements. Undocumented opcodes, precise instruction timing, interrupt
topology, and peripheral response belong to named profiles unless separately
promoted by evidence and policy.
