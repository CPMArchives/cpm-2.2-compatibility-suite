Investigation 063 documentation notes (paraphrase and bounded extracts)

CP/M 2.0 Interface Guide, introduction:
- Standard CP/M assumes an Intel MDS-800 reference development system.
- Hardware dependence is isolated in the BIOS; CCP and BDOS are presented as
  hardware-independent modules.

CP/M 2.2 Alteration Guide, reserved page zero:
- 0000h contains a jump to warm start; 0005h contains a jump to BDOS.
- 0003h is identified as the Intel standard IOBYTE.
- 0008h-0037h are identified as interrupt/restart locations, with 0038h used
  by DDT/SID for programmed breakpoints when active.

CP/M 2.2 Alteration Guide, BIOS and disk adaptation:
- BIOS interfaces are expressed with 8080 register pairs and operations.
- Sector skew may be changed for faster processors, memory, and disks. This
  documents configurability rather than a standard processor clock.

The manuals define CP/M-visible register call conventions but do not promise:
- Z80 IX/IY, alternate registers, I, or R at transient entry or across BDOS;
- Z80-only or undocumented opcode execution;
- a processor clock rate or instruction-cycle timing contract;
- a universal interrupt controller or interrupt-mode topology.
