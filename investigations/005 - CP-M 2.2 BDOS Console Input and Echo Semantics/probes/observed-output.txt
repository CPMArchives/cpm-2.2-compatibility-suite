Investigation 005 raw observed output
=====================================

Record format:

  returned-byte  CONOUT-count  first-eight-CONOUT-bytes
  LIST-count     BIOS-CONIN-call-count

Bytes beyond CONOUT-count are stale capture storage and are not evidence.

Printer echo initially inactive
-------------------------------

A>IN005

IN005 ret conlen con[8] listlen conincalls
A    41 01 41 FF FF FF FF FF FF FF 00 01
TAB  09 08 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01
CR   0D 01 0D 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01
LF   0A 01 0A 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01
BS   08 01 08 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01
C-A  01 00 08 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01
C-PQ 10 01 51 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01
PEND status 01 5A 01 5A 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01

Interpretation:

  A returned 41h and echoed one 41h byte.
  TAB returned 09h and echoed eight spaces from known column zero.
  CR, LF, and backspace returned and echoed unchanged.
  Ctrl-A returned 01h and emitted no echo.
  Ctrl-P returned 10h and emitted no echo. The following formatted Q emitted
  only to CONOUT, so function 1 did not toggle printer echo.
  Function 11 returned 01h after consuming scripted Z into DRI pending state;
  function 1 then returned and echoed Z with the total BIOS CONIN count still
  one, proving it consumed the retained byte without a second BIOS input.

Printer echo active before program entry
----------------------------------------

Ctrl-P was entered at the CCP prompt, using the already-established DRI
buffered-input mechanism, before invoking IN005.

A>IN005

IN005 ret conlen con[8] listlen conincalls
A    41 01 41 FF FF FF FF FF FF FF 01 01
TAB  09 08 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 08 01
CR   0D 01 0D 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 01 01
LF   0A 01 0A 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 01 01
BS   08 01 08 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 01 01
C-A  01 00 08 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 00 01
C-PQ 10 01 51 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 01 01
PEND status 01 5A 01 5A 20 20 20 20 20 20 20 01 01

Interpretation:

  Every eligible echoed byte was also sent to LIST after formatting. TAB
  produced eight LIST calls for its eight expanded spaces. Non-echoed Ctrl-A
  and Ctrl-P produced no LIST call. The Q following Ctrl-P was still copied to
  LIST, consistent with function 1 leaving the pre-existing echo state active
  rather than toggling it.

Rejected development runs
-------------------------

Two development runs exposed probe-reporting defects: one allowed a diagnostic
BDOS call to clobber the record pointer; the next cleared the scripted input
byte before the call. Their output was rejected, the instrumentation was fixed,
and neither run contributes evidence. The final source and binary correspond to
the two valid outputs preserved above.
