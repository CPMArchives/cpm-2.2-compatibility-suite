EXTENSION VALIDATION POLICY

1. Declare the baseline/strict CP/M personality, configured platform and every
   optional/hardware/extension profile before testing.
2. Run all applicable narrow I052 tests before and after enabling the extension.
3. Run the I053 corpus workflows applicable to the affected surfaces.
4. Compare raw registers, memory, FCB/DMA, console/BIOS activity, files and images;
   do not accept semantic drift merely because a modern abstraction seems better.
5. For REQUIRED entries, demand observational equivalence.
6. For POLICY PENDING entries, keep tests inactive/diagnostic unless the profile is
   selected, then require its explicit expected result.
7. For NOT GUARANTEED entries, verify the extension does not advertise one observed
   result as a new baseline guarantee.
8. For NOT REQUIRED entries, ensure acceptance does not depend on reproducing DRI
   internals; alternate extension mechanisms must pass.
9. Test disabled and enabled extension states. Returning to CP/M must restore a
   coherent personality state and must not leave hidden namespace/device mutations.
10. Preserve exact extension/version/profile manifests and artifact hashes.
